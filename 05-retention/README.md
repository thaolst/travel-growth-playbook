# 🔄 05 — Retention

The first booking is acquisition. The second booking is where retention begins.

## 📄 Files

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| [booking1-to-booking2-playbook.md](./booking1-to-booking2-playbook.md) | 90-day playbook after the first booking — the most important window in travel retention |

## 💡 Key insight

If there's no deliberate mechanic to bring users back within 60-90 days of their first booking, most won't return on their own. Not because they're dissatisfied — but because they have no specific reason to choose your platform over another next time.
