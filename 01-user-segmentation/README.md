# 🎯 01 — User Segmentation

Segment travel users by motivation, not demographics.

## 📄 Files

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| [travel-segment-framework.md](./travel-segment-framework.md) | 4 segments that actually matter: Connection Seeker, Relaxation Seeker, Family Traveler, Explorer |

## 💡 Key insight

Knowing your user is 25-34 with high income doesn't help you write a campaign.

Knowing they're traveling to reconnect with someone they love, or to decompress after a brutal month — that's what determines what they respond to.
