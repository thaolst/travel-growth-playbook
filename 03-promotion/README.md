# 🎁 03 — Promotion Mechanics

ATL, BTL, partnership, gamification, loyalty — not all of them work in travel. This framework helps pick the right mechanic for the right user at the right time.

## 📄 Files

| File | Content |
|---|---|

## 💡 Key insight

The problem with most travel promotions isn't that the offer is too small. It's that the right offer reaches the wrong person.

A 30% voucher sent to someone about to book anyway is just margin loss. The same voucher sent to someone on the fence is conversion.
