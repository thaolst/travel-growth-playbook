# 📊 Benchmarks & Industry Data

Tham khảo cho travel growth marketing tại Việt Nam. Dữ liệu tổng hợp từ công bố công khai, báo cáo ngành và kinh nghiệm thực chiến.

> **⚠️ Lưu ý:** Các số liệu là ước tính dựa trên thông tin công khai và kinh nghiệm. Số thực tế phụ thuộc vào platform, segment, và thị trường cụ thể. Dùng làm reference để calibrate, không phải truth tuyệt đối.

---

## 1. Retention & Churn Benchmarks

### Travel platform retention (ngành, ước tính)

| Metric | Vietnam Travel | Global Travel | E-commerce (reference) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeat booking rate (within 6 months) | 15-25% | 20-30% | 40-60% |
| Single-booking user ratio | 55-70% | 50-60% | 20-35% |
| Average bookings/user/year | 2-3 | 2.5-4 | 8-15 |
| Time between booking 1 → 2 (median) | 75-120 days | 60-90 days | 14-30 days |
| Drop-off booking 1 → 2 | 60-75% | 50-65% | 30-45% |
| Drop-off booking 2 → 3 | 40-55% | 35-45% | 20-30% |

**Nguồn tham khảo:** Báo cáo ngành từ McKinsey Travel (2023-2025), BCG Travel Loyalty Report, Phocuswright.

### Segment-level retention (ước tính)

| Segment | Repeat rate (6mo) | Key churn window | Best retention lever |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connection Seeker | 20-30% | 60-90 days | Social trigger + companion offer |
| Relaxation Seeker | 25-35% | 90-120 days | Upgrade mechanic + loyalty tier |
| Family Traveler | 30-40% | Before next school holiday | Seasonal timing + bundle |
| Explorer | 10-20% | 45-60 days | Gamification + exclusive inventory |

## 2. Promotion Performance Benchmarks

### By mechanic type

| Mechanic | Avg redemption rate | CAC impact | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATL (flash deal, voucher) | 3-8% | Medium reduction | New user acquisition |
| BTL (behavioral trigger) | 12-25% | High reduction | Current user conversion |
| Partnership (exclusive offer) | 8-18% | Medium reduction | Brand credibility + retention |
| Gamification (lucky draw, streak) | 15-30% (engagement) | Low reduction | Engagement, frequency building |
| Loyalty program (tier, points) | 5-10% (point earn) | Long-term improvement | High-value segment retention |

### By segment — offer sensitivity

| Segment | Small offer (5-10%) | Medium (15-25%) | Large (30%+) | Non-monetary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Connection Seeker | Low | Medium | Medium-High | High (experience upgrade) |
| Relaxation Seeker | Low | Low-Medium | Medium | Very High (room upgrade, lounge) |
| Family Traveler | Medium | High | High | Low (prefer cash value) |
| Explorer | Very Low | Medium | Very High | Medium (exclusive inventory) |

## 4. Budget Allocation Benchmarks

### Annual budget split (mature travel platform)

| Category | % of total budget |
|---|---|
| Performance marketing (Google/Meta/Social) | 35-45% |
| Promotional (voucher, discount, flash deal) | 20-30% |
| Brand & content | 10-15% |
| Partnership co-marketing | 10-15% |
| Retention & loyalty | 5-10% |
| Product & engineering (growth features) | 5-10% |

### By lifecycle stage

| Stage | Acquisition | Retention | Brand |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch (0-12 months) | 70-80% | 5-10% | 10-20% |
| Growth (1-3 years) | 50-60% | 15-20% | 15-20% |
| Mature (3+ years) | 35-45% | 25-30% | 20-25% |

## 5. Conversion Rate Benchmarks

### Funnel by vertical (estimated)

| Stage | Airline | Bus | Train | Hotel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search → View detail | 25-35% | 30-40% | 30-40% | 20-30% |
| View detail → Add to cart | 15-25% | 20-30% | 20-30% | 10-20% |
| Add to cart → Payment start | 50-65% | 55-70% | 55-70% | 40-55% |
| Payment start → Completed | 60-75% | 65-80% | 65-80% | 55-70% |
| **Overall (search → book)** | **2-5%** | **3-7%** | **3-7%** | **1-3%** |

### Payment drop-off reasons (Vietnam OTA)

| Reason | % of payment failures |
|---|---|
| Payment method not supported | 30-40% |
| Technical error (timeout, gateway) | 20-30% |
| User changed mind / price check | 15-25% |
| Price changed during flow | 10-15% |
| Voucher/offer invalid at payment | 5-10% |
