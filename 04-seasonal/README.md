# 📅 04 — Seasonal Campaign Calendar

Travel demand in Vietnam follows predictable seasonal patterns. Know the pattern to know when to push acquisition, when to push retention, and when to pull back.

## 📄 Files

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| [vietnam-campaign-calendar.md](./vietnam-campaign-calendar.md) | Campaign calendar across 4 phases of the year + monthly budget allocation |

## 💡 Key insight

October is the lowest demand month of the year and CAC is highest. Don't pour budget into conversion campaigns — use it to build SEO, clean your CRM, and develop your loyalty program.
